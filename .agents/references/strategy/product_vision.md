# Site Vision — trevillyanlabs.io

The authoritative statement of what this website is *for*. Page copy, design, and feature
decisions defer to this doc. Company positioning is canonical in
`docs/company/identity-and-positioning.md`; this doc translates that positioning into a website.

## One-line vision

> **Trevillyan Labs ships custom software for clients, operates its own products, and advises founders
> and early-stage startups on product and go-to-market execution.**

The site's **one job is to get visitors to hire the studio** — custom builds and product/GTM
advisory. The studio's own products (NewsNook) appear as **proof** that TL ships and runs real
software, and link out to their own marketing sites; **selling the products themselves is each
product's site's job (e.g. newsnook.ai), not this one's.** Primary CTA everywhere: *Work with us*.

**Hero copy (baseline — A/B-test variants in PostHog):**

> ### We build and run software — ours and yours.
> Trevillyan Labs ships custom software for clients, operates its own products (our app **NewsNook**
> is live today), and advises founders and early-stage startups on **product and go-to-market
> execution**. A studio that builds and *runs* its own software brings that same ownership to yours.
>
> **[Work with us]   ·   [See what we ship → NewsNook]**

**Supporting line (a differentiator, not the headline):** the studio runs lean on AI — operated
day-to-day by Ren, an AI assistant — so we execute fast and we practice the AI leverage we help
clients adopt. Use this on Services/advisory and a "how we work" beat, not the hero.

## Expanded vision — what we offer

Three ways to work with Trevillyan Labs. Client value leads; the owned products prove the studio
builds and *runs* real software (not a contract-and-walk-away shop). Service copy is drafted per page
in Phase 2; this is the source of truth for it.

### Build — custom software, shipped and run

- **Contract software development.** Full-stack web apps in the studio's own stack (Next.js / React /
  TypeScript; Python for scripting and automation) — MVPs, internal tools, and custom products taken
  from idea to production. For founders and operators who need software built but don't have a team.
  *Proof:* the studio builds and operates its own product (NewsNook) on this exact stack.
- **Web & portfolio builds.** Focused, high-craft marketing and portfolio sites, handled end-to-end
  (design → build → maintain). For authors, creators, and small teams who want to look credible online
  without running a project themselves. *Proof:* this site, and a journalism portfolio build (client
  unnamed publicly).

### Advise — product & go-to-market execution

The focus is **product and go-to-market execution** — not "AI advisory." AI is a *means* of executing
lean and the studio's operating differentiator (operated by Ren), not the headline of this offering.

- **Startup & founder advisory (the offering).** Hands-on help for founders and early-stage startups
  on what decides whether a company makes it: product strategy and the path to product-market fit,
  go-to-market, fundraising, and team building — running lean with agentic AI as a means, not the
  pitch. From a 3x founder who ships a live product, not a career consultant.
- **Applying AI for leaders (secondary).** TL also advises leaders on where agents, assistants,
  automation, and tooling fit (and what they can't do) — a secondary line, not a headline pillar, and
  kept off the hero. *Proof:* the studio itself runs on agentic AI.

### Products — owned software (the credibility engine)

- **NewsNook.** The studio's flagship owned product — "the AI newsletter reader for thought leaders" —
  live in production at newsnook.ai. It's both a product buyers can use *and* the clearest proof TL
  ships and operates real software. Indie-SaaS know-how earned here feeds every client build and
  advisory engagement, and recurring product revenue is the studio's north star.

## Why the site exists (jobs to be done)

1. **Convert visitors into studio clients (priority 1).** Custom builds and product/GTM advisory →
   a qualified, intent-routed contact or booked call. This is the site's primary conversion goal.
   Owned products are shown as proof and link out to their own sites; **selling the products is those
   sites' job, not this one's.**
2. **Establish credibility fast.** A studio that ships its own products, has real client work, and
   holds issued patents — not a freelancer, not a bloated agency. NewsNook is the centerpiece of this
   proof (a live product, linked out), not a sales funnel here.
3. **Be the portfolio piece.** The site is itself the strongest sample of TL's work. A fast, tasteful,
   modern Next.js site *is* the pitch: "we'll build yours like this."
4. **Be found and quoted correctly (SEO / AEO / LLM).** Win inbound from search engines *and* AI
   answer engines/LLMs — be the cited, accurately-represented answer when prospects (or their AI
   assistants) look for a studio to build or advise. A primary acquisition channel and a credibility
   proof for an AI-native studio. Full objectives + technical means in **Discoverability** below.
5. **Compound over time.** A growing SEO/AEO surface + thought-leadership (later, the blog) so the
   site earns inbound rather than relying on outreach — aligned with the studio's leverage-over-hours
   model.

## Positioning & credibility (the case the site must make)

Offerings are above; this is *why believe us*, in order:

1. **We deliver client value.** Lead with the work clients buy — custom software and product/GTM
   execution help.
2. **We're real builders, not a pass-through.** TL runs **two engines** — it builds and operates its
   **own products** (NewsNook, live) alongside **client work**. A studio that ships its own software
   is proof it can ship yours, and that it stays to *run* what it builds.
3. **We run lean on AI.** The studio operates day-to-day on an AI assistant (Ren) — so we execute
   fast, at senior quality, without agency overhead, and we live the AI leverage we advise on. A
   supporting differentiator (strongest on the advisory lines), not the headline.
4. **Track record.** Issued patents and shipped work back it up — credibility proof, not the headline.
   (The old "invent and license patents" story is retired.)

## Discoverability — SEO, AEO & LLM objectives

Trevillyan Labs is an AI-native studio, so the site must be found *and accurately represented* by
search engines, **AI answer engines** (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, etc.), and
the LLMs prospects increasingly use to shortlist who to hire. Being citable and correct in those
channels is a primary acquisition lever — and, fittingly, a credibility proof in its own right. Three
objectives:

1. **Drive client referrals & customers (acquisition).** When a prospect — or their AI assistant —
   asks "who can build my MVP," "a studio that ships Next.js products," "someone to advise our startup
   on product and AI," TL should surface, get recommended, and earn the click. Target high-intent
   queries per offering (build · product & go-to-market advisory · web builds; applying-AI secondary)
   and the
   founder's name/entity.
2. **Convey credibility accurately (representation).** Make engines and LLMs describe TL correctly and
   favorably from **docs-supported facts**: an independent software studio that builds and advises,
   ships its own live product (NewsNook), holds issued patents, runs lean on AI. Prevent hallucination,
   the stale "patent-lab" framing, and underselling — give models a canonical, machine-readable source
   of truth so they quote us rather than guess.
3. **Win the AI-mediated buyer journey.** More buyers research and shortlist via LLMs/answer engines
   before they ever reach the site. Be the answer that gets cited — then convert on arrival into the
   primary funnel (contact/booking).

**How the site delivers it (technical objectives):**

- **SEO:** Next.js SSR/SSG, per-route metadata + canonical URLs, dynamic `sitemap.xml` + `robots.txt`,
  top-tier Core Web Vitals, semantic HTML, sensible internal linking, per-page OG images.
- **AEO (answer-engine optimization):** concise, extractable answers and an **FAQ that mirrors how
  prospects actually ask** ("can a small studio build my MVP?", "who advises founders on AI?");
  question-shaped headings; quotable, self-contained claims.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD):** `Organization`, `Person` (founder), `Service` (per offering),
  `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Article` (case studies/blog) — to feed knowledge graphs and answer
  engines.
- **LLM / crawler-friendliness:** publish an **`llms.txt`** (curated, accurate overview — what TL does,
  the offerings, proof points, how to engage, contact) and clean server-rendered text; **allow all
  reputable AI crawlers — training *and* answer/search** (GPTBot, ClaudeBot/anthropic-ai, CCBot,
  Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, …) in `robots.txt`, so LLMs both
  natively *know* TL and cite it live (decision + bot list: `decisions/ADR-0004-allow-ai-crawlers.md`);
  keep **entity consistency** (link out to LinkedIn, the patents, newsnook.ai) for disambiguation.
- **Truth constraint:** everything exposed to crawlers and LLMs states only docs-supported facts —
  accuracy *is* the objective (see Principles). No invented metrics or credentials.

**Measurement:** organic + AI-referral traffic to studio pages; branded/entity search lift;
spot-checked appearance/citation in answer engines for target prompts; all feeding the primary funnel
(→ contact/booking). Tracked in PostHog where attributable (see `../integrations.md`).

## Success metrics (what "working" means)

- **Primary:** qualified, intent-routed contact submissions and booked calls — i.e. studio leads.
- **Secondary:** case-study engagement; returning visitors; NewsNook click-throughs (a proof/interest
  signal, *not* a primary goal — the product converts on newsnook.ai).
- **Discoverability:** organic + AI-referral traffic to studio pages; branded/entity search lift;
  spot-checked citation in AI answer engines for target prompts (see **Discoverability** above).
- **Instrumented in PostHog** as the core funnel: visitor → service view → contact/booking. (See
  `../integrations.md`.)
- **Quality bar:** top-tier Core Web Vitals + Lighthouse, full structured data, valid `llms.txt` +
  `robots.txt` + `sitemap.xml`, zero console errors.

## Scope guardrails

- **Must ship (v1):** the studio story (home, hire-first), Services, a NewsNook proof page (credibility
  + link out to newsnook.ai — not a product sales page), Work (4 case studies; KPMG held back), About/founder, Patents
  (preserved), Contact (intent-routed, Turnstile), legal — **plus the discoverability layer**: per-page
  metadata + JSON-LD, an FAQ for AEO, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` (AI crawlers allowed), and
  `llms.txt`.
- **Deferred (Phase 5):** blog/writing engine, on-site AI assistant, build-in-public live metrics.
  These are committed in direction but not v1 launch blockers (see `../../../REVAMP-PLAN.md` §5–6).
- **Out of scope:** anything that exposes confidential client terms, pricing, or prospect-stage work
  (see `docs/company/identity-and-positioning.md §Confidentiality`).

## Principles

- **Respect the visitor's time** — the brand voice is direct and product-minded; the site should be
  too. Every page answers "what is this and what do I do next" above the fold.
- **Show, don't claim** — proof (shipped product, real clients, patents, the site itself) over
  adjectives.
- **One obvious next action per page.**
- **Truth only** — never publish unverified facts (e.g. no invented credentials); state only what the
  company docs support.
